### 설명

```
모아는 행사에서 찍은 사진을 그 자리에서 모으는 앱입니다.

행사가 끝나면 사진을 주고받는 일이 늘 숙제로 남습니다. 단체 대화방에 올린 사진은 화질이 떨어지고, 찍은 날짜도 잃어버립니다. 모아는 QR 하나로 이 과정을 끝냅니다.

쓰는 방법

1. 이벤트를 만들고 QR을 화면에 띄웁니다.
2. 사진을 보낼 사람이 iPhone 카메라로 QR을 찍습니다. 앱을 설치하지 않아도 됩니다.
3. 보낼 사진과 동영상을 고릅니다.
4. 사진이 내 사진 앱에 바로 들어옵니다.

모아의 특징

설치가 필요 없습니다. 보내는 사람은 App Clip으로 보냅니다. 가입도 로그인도 없습니다.

원본 그대로 옵니다. HEIC와 라이브 포토, 동영상을 압축하지 않고 그대로 받습니다.

앨범으로 정리됩니다. 이벤트 이름과 같은 앨범이 사진 앱에 만들어지고, 받은 사진이 거기에 모입니다.

누가 보냈는지 보입니다. 보내는 사람이 이름을 적으면 사진마다 함께 표시됩니다.
```

### Description

```
Moa collects the photos from your event while everyone is still there.

Getting photos back after a gathering is always a chore. Group chats compress them and lose the date they were taken. Moa settles it with a single QR code.

How it works

1. Create an event and show its QR code.
2. Guests scan it with the iPhone camera. There is no app to install.
3. They pick the photos and videos they want to send.
4. The photos land in your Photos app right away.

What makes Moa different

Nothing to install. Guests send through an App Clip. No account, no sign in.

Originals arrive intact. HEIC, Live Photos and videos come through without being compressed.

They sit at the right date. Received photos land at the moment they were taken, not at today, so they mix in with your own shots.

Sorted into an album. An album named after your event is created in Photos, and everything you receive collects there.

You see who sent what. If a guest types their name, it shows next to their photos.

No server holding your photos. On the same Wi-Fi, photos go straight from their iPhone to yours. Only when that fails do they take an encrypted detour through iCloud, and they are deleted the moment you receive them.

Good for

Weddings and receptions
Birthdays and family gatherings
Club meetups and workshops
Trips and group outings
Classes and talks

Good to know

Keep the event screen open while photos are arriving.
To receive directly, both iPhones need to be on the same network.
A new QR code is generated each time you open the screen. Older codes stop working.
```

### Review notes

```
This app receives photos from another iPhone on the same Wi-Fi network. Testing it requires two iPhones on the same network.

Host
1. Open the app and create an event. Allow photo access and local network access.
2. Open the event to show its QR code. This screen must stay open to receive.

Guest
3. Scan that QR code with the camera on the second iPhone. An App Clip opens.
4. Pick photos, and they are imported into the host iPhone's Photos app.

When the two devices cannot reach each other, the app encrypts the files and passes them through the developer's CloudKit public database briefly, deleting each record as soon as the host receives it. The decryption key exists only inside the QR code.
```
